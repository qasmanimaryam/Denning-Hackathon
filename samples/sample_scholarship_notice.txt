Programme Name: Sindh IT & Emerging Sciences Talent Scholarship 2026-27
Provider: Information, Science & Technology Department, Government of Sindh
Official Portal: https://istd.sindh.gov.pk/scholarships

1. Overview & Benefits:
The Government of Sindh invites applications from talented and deserving students for the Sindh IT & Emerging Sciences Talent Scholarship 2026-27. Selected candidates will receive Full Funding covering 100% university tuition fees plus a monthly stipend of PKR 15,000 for the academic year.

2. Eligibility Criteria:
- Domicile / Province: Applicants must hold a valid Domicile and PRC of Sindh province.
- Degree Level: Open to Undergraduate (BS / BE) and Masters (MS) students.
- Eligible Fields of Study: Computer Science (CS), Software Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Information Technology, and Electrical Engineering.
- Minimum Academic Marks: Candidates must have secured at least 70% marks in their last Intermediate / Semester examination or a minimum CGPA of 2.80 out of 4.00.
- Family Income Limit: Maximum monthly family income from all sources must not exceed PKR 65,000 per month.
- Other Conditions: Must be enrolled as a regular full-time student in an HEC-recognized public sector university and must not be availing any other merit or need-based scholarship.

3. Required Documents:
- Applicant CNIC or NADRA B-Form
- Sindh Domicile and PRC (Form-C)
- Intermediate HSSC Marksheet and Latest University Transcript
- University Bonafide / Enrollment Certificate
- Father / Guardian Monthly Income Certificate or Salary Slip
- Last 3 Months Electricity & Gas Utility Bills

4. Important Dates:
- Applications Open Date: 2026-10-01
- Last Date to Apply (Confirmed Deadline): 2026-11-15
- How to Apply: Submit the online form at https://istd.sindh.gov.pk/scholarships/apply before 15 November 2026. No agent or middleman fee is charged at any stage.
